After csynth, open the Performance & Resource table and compare each module with its estimate. For a layer that is far over:
- loops with Pipelined = no around a pipelined loop (EXP-002);
- extra small loops under the pixel loop (EXP-001);
- Interval far above the trip count of a pipelined loop (II violation, HLS 200-880).
Fix the structure before adding lanes; adding lanes does not remove fill/drain cost.
Observed in a past LeNet run: estimate about 12,000-15,000 cycles with the chosen lanes, csynth 72,011.
