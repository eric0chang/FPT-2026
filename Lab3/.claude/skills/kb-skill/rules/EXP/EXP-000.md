Rewriting the structure from scratch loses what made the past design meet timing, II and the resource limits; adapting it keeps that.
- Copy the functions you reuse, then change types and the number of parallel MACs (lanes) to fit the task's limits.
- Keep its loop nest shape (which loops are pipelined, flattened, unrolled), its buffer shapes and partitioning, and the config lines of its run_hls.tcl unless the task forces a change.
Observed in past LeNet runs (float KB design -> fixed-point task):
- the agent said it reused the KB line-buffer convolution but rewrote it: 58 cycles per pixel instead of about 15 (csynth 72,011 cycles for an architecture that fits in about 15,000);
- the (K-1)-row line buffer replaced by a completely partitioned flat shift register: first csynth LUT 30.7 %, over the 30 % limit (24.9 % when kept);
- `config_compile -enable_auto_rewind=false` left out of run_hls.tcl: `HLS 200-656` on the first csynth.
