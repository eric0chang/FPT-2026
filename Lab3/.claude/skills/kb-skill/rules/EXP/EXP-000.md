Rewriting the structure from scratch loses what made the past design meet timing and II; adapting it keeps that.
- Copy the functions you reuse, then change types and the number of parallel MACs (lanes) to fit the task's limits.
- Keep its loop nest shape (which loops are pipelined, flattened, unrolled) unless the task forces a change.
Observed in a past LeNet run (float KB design -> fixed-point task): the agent said it reused the KB line-buffer convolution but rewrote it; the rewrite took 58 cycles per pixel instead of about 15 (csynth 72,011 cycles for an architecture that fits in about 15,000).
