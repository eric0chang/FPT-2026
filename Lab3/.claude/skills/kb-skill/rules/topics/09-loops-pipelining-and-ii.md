# Topic 9: Loops, pipelining and II (34 rules)

Covers: PIPELINE, II, UNROLL, loop-carried dependences, DEPENDENCE, LOOP_FLATTEN/MERGE, trip counts, PERFORMANCE. `must` / `must-not` first. Full rule: `../HLS/<ID>.md`.
