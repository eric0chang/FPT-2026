- `register` puts registers on a port's data and protocol signals and keeps them valid through the function's final cycle or later; it applies to s_axilite, ap_fifo, ap_none, ap_hs, ap_ack, ap_vld and ap_ovld.
- config_interface -register_io registers all top-level I/O globally.

Source: UG1399 v2023.2, Ch.16 HLS Pragmas > pragma HLS interface, pp. 527-528.
