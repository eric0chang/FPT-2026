These are stated limits of the free-running pipeline style. Outside a DATAFLOW region, use stp or flp, or let the tool choose the style.

Source: UG1399 v2023.2, Ch.3 Loops Primer > Flushing Pipelines and Pipeline Types, pp. 67-68.
