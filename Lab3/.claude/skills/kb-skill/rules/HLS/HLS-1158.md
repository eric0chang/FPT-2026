- The scheduler places each operation in a cycle based on when its inputs are ready, the clock period, the device-specific operator delay, available resources and user directives.
- Trade-off: longer period -> fewer cycles but lower frequency; shorter period -> operations spread over more cycles.

Source: UG1399 v2023.2, Ch.10 Optimizing Techniques and Troubleshooting Tips, pp. 249-250.
