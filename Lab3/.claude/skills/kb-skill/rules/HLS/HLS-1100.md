- Without side-channels: very light interface with the fewest resources.
- With side-channels: full-featured interface, more control signals (e.g. TLAST, TUSER).
- Handshake: the producer raises TVALID, the consumer answers with TREADY; beats continue until one of them drops or TLAST flags the final beat.
- Customize the data by overloading TDATA with your type rather than redefining the signal struct.

Source: UG1399 v2023.2, Ch.8 Interfaces of the HLS Design > Details of AXIS Interfaces for Vitis, pp. 156-157.
