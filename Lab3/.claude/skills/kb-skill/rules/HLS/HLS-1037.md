rewind overlaps successive executions of a pipelined loop, and flp lets the pipeline drain when input stops. The manual makes their compatibility conditional on syn.compile.enable_auto_rewind being turned on.

Source: UG1399 v2023.2, Ch.3 Loops Primer > Flushing Pipelines and Pipeline Types, p. 68.
