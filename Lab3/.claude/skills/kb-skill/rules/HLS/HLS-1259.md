- Without II=, HLS aims for the minimum II compatible with the clock period, putting timing ahead of II; state II= when a throughput target matters.
- Loop-carried dependencies can block pipelining altogether or force a larger interval.
- The DEPENDENCE pragma gives the tool additional information about such dependencies so the loop can be pipelined, or pipelined with a lower II.

Source: UG1399 v2023.2, Ch.16 HLS Pragmas > pragma HLS pipeline, pp. 540-541.
