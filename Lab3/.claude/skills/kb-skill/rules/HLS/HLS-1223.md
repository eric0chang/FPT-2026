- Symptom: more multipliers than `limit=` show up, coming from called functions.
- Cause: the limit's scope excludes operators outside the function, including those inside its sub-functions.
- Fix: repeat `#pragma HLS allocation operation instances=mul limit=1` in each callee, or inline the callees so their operators fall under the caller's limit.

Source: UG1399 v2023.2, Ch.16 HLS Pragmas > pragma HLS allocation, p. 496.
