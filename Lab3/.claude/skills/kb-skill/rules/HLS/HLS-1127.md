Register modes: forward (TDATA and TVALID registered), reverse (TREADY only), both (all three, the default) and off (none). Side-channel signals count as data and are registered whenever TDATA is. Turning registering off on both ends of a link removes this protection.

Source: UG1399 v2023.2, Ch.8 Interfaces of the HLS Design > Registered AXI4-Stream Interfaces, p. 196.
