- Symptom: a configured active-High reset (syn.rtl.reset_level defaults to active-High) does not appear in the generated RTL, and synthesis of a design with m_axi / s_axilite / axis ports warns about the reset level.
- Cause: the AXI4 standard mandates an active-Low reset, so HLS overrides the config_rtl polarity.
- Fix: treat the reset as active-Low for any design with AXI4 ports.
Also covered in UG1399 Ch.8 Interfaces of the HLS Design > Interfaces for Vitis Kernel Flow, p. 152-163.

Source: UG1399 v2023.2, Ch.8 Interfaces of the HLS Design > Controlling Initialization and Reset Behavior, p. 241.
