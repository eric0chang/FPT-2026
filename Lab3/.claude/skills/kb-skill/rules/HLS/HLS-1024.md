- The tool infers processes and inserts channels plus synchronization; throughput is then limited only by free space in input/output buffers, and control fanout is split across per-process controllers instead of one central stall.
- In the manual's diamond example (A feeds B and C, which feed D), one call drops from 475 to 275 cycles with FIFO channels.
- The overlap is not observable statically: check the cosim timeline; the Dataflow Viewer after C synthesis only shows the structure.

Source: UG1399 v2023.2, Ch.2 Abstract Parallel Programming Model for HLS > Control-driven Task-level Parallelism, pp. 48-50.
