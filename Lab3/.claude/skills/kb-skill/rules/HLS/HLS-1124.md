summary: axis data is padded to whole bytes (a 12-bit type becomes 16 bits, sign- or zero-extended by signedness) and a struct on an axis port is aligned to its widest member, so expect TDATA wider than the sum of member widths.

- struct {char p; char q;} packs into 2 bytes; struct {int p; char q;} is aligned to 4 bytes, so q gets 3 pad bytes and the struct takes 8 bytes.
- Structs are kept aggregated into one wide word by default, which is how several variables can share one AXI4-Stream.
- Mixing one wide member with narrow ones therefore spends TDATA bits on padding.

Source: UG1399 v2023.2, Ch.8 Interfaces of the HLS Design > AXI4-Stream Interfaces, pp. 193-197.
