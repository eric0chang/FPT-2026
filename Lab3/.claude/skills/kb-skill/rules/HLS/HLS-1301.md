summary: Fixed-point hls_math functions support only ap_fixed<W,I> with I<=33 and W-I<=32, ap_ufixed<W,I> with I<=32 and W-I<=32, ap_int<I> with I<=33, ap_uint<I> with I<=32, and do not support the Q, O, N template parameters.

- Q, O and N are the quantization mode, overflow mode and number of saturation bits.
- Those modes take effect only when an `ap_[u]fixed` variable is assigned or initialized, not during a calculation, so they play no part inside the math function; they apply when its result is assigned to a variable declared with them.

Source: UG1399 v2023.2, Ch.22 HLS Math Library > Fixed-Point Math Functions, p. 728.
