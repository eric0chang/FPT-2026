- The syn folders hold the top-level RTL, one file per non-inlined sub-function and helper blocks (RAMs, pipelined multipliers), yet may still be incomplete; the impl/verilog and impl/vhdl folders hold the IP-creation scripts instead of the IP.
- Packaging the RTL produces the complete output to use downstream, and the IP generation step happens automatically.
- The manual flags direct use of the raw RTL folders as something to avoid but allows it (p.395): if you copy the files, running their scripts correctly during RTL synthesis becomes your job.
Also covered in UG1399 Ch.12 Building and Running an HLS Component > Packaging the RTL Design, p. 395.

Source: UG1399 v2023.2, Ch.12 Building and Running an HLS Component > Running C Synthesis > Output of C Synthesis, pp. 360-361.
