- Symptom: cosim reports FAIL on small numeric differences against the C simulation results.
- Math functions: C sim runs the host math library, while the RTL uses a bit-approximate implementation that may even use another algorithm. Whether the residual error is acceptable is a design decision; to have C sim reproduce the hardware numbers instead, switch to `hls_math.h` (C++ only).
- `syn.compile.unsafe_math_optimizations` lets the tool reorder float/double operations (expression balancing) and also turns on `no_signed_zeros`, which treats e.g. `x + 0.0` and `x`, or `x * 0.0` and `0.0`, as equal; `no_signed_zeros` can also be enabled by itself.
- Fix: check results within a range. One way: keep two identical copies of the function, one synthesized and one software-only reference, and let `main()` flag any output whose absolute difference exceeds your tolerance; in C sim both copies agree, in co-sim the check measures the RTL error.
```cpp
if (std::fabs(hw_val - golden) > eps) mismatches++;
```
