- Total latency = iteration latency + II x (number of items - 1).
- Pipelining raises throughput (rate of new results after the first) but leaves the time one item needs to traverse the system unchanged.
- Manual's example: three 20-minute stages give the first result after 60 min and then one every 20 min, so three items take 100 min instead of 180 sequentially.

Source: UG1399 v2023.2, Ch.1 Design Principles > Pipelining Paradigm, pp. 31-32.
