- After reset the block waits for ap_start; ap_idle falls as soon as it starts, and ap_ready stays inactive until then.
- At the one-cycle ap_ready pulse: ap_start still High starts the next transaction at once (ap_idle stays Low); ap_start Low lets the current run finish and halt, with ap_idle rising one cycle after ap_done, unless ap_start rises again before ap_done.
- A pipelined block can pulse ap_ready any cycle after ap_start is sampled, so logic expecting ap_ready and ap_done together breaks once the block is pipelined.
- ap_continue (ap_ctrl_chain; tied to 1 in ap_ctrl_hs): High at ap_done lets runs go back to back; Low makes the block stop with ap_done held High until ap_continue rises.
- Port-level I/O protocols on data ports are independent of this handshake.

Source: UG1399 v2023.2, Ch.8 Interfaces of the HLS Design > Execution Modes of HLS Designs > Block-Level Control Protocols > ap_ctrl_chain, pp. 230-232.
