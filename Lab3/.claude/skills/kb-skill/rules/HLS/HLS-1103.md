- ap_fifo serves both a top-level stream port and a channel between functions inside the IP.
- With hls::stream the accesses are in order by construction.

Source: UG1399 v2023.2, Ch.8 Interfaces of the HLS Design > AP_FIFO in the Vivado IP Flow, pp. 158-162.
