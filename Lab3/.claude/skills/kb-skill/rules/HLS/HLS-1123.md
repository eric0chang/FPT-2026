---
id: HLS-1123
summary: Do not use hls::axis, ap_axiu or ap_axis on internal functions or local variables; the AXI4-Stream protocol exists only on top-level function interfaces. Use hls::stream for streams inside the design.
tags:
  - ap_axiu
  - axis
  - group:hlskb
  - hls-stream
  - tool:vitis-hls

records: []
---
These side-channel packet types describe the top-level port protocol. Between internal functions (e.g. DATAFLOW processes) declare hls::stream of an ordinary element type instead.

Source: UG1399 v2023.2, Ch.8 Interfaces of the HLS Design > AXI4-Stream Interfaces, p. 193.
