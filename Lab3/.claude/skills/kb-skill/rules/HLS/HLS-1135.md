---
id: HLS-1135
summary: Do not use ap_ack on an output port if the design must be verified with C/RTL co-simulation; co-simulation cannot verify designs that use ap_ack on outputs.
tags:

With ap_ack on an output, HLS adds an input acknowledge and stalls after each write until it is High, but no output signal says when data may be consumed. Use ap_vld or ap_hs on outputs when co-simulation is part of verification.

Source: UG1399 v2023.2, Ch.8 Interfaces of the HLS Design > Port-Level I/O: Wire Handshakes, p. 207.
