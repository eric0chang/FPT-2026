- DEPENDENCE gives HLS extra information that removes a loop-carried dependency it would otherwise honour, enabling pipelining or a smaller interval.
- Only valid when the reordered access cannot corrupt data you care about, e.g. initial contents that may be thrown away.

Source: UG1399 v2023.2, Ch.10 Optimizing Techniques and Troubleshooting Tips, pp. 246-247.
