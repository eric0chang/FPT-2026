- Cause: the 2023.2 timing model was retuned so HLS estimates sit closer to Vivado; the main changes concern the delay of AXI4-Stream interfaces and of complicated enable logic for pipeline control.
- Fix, when the design previously closed timing in both HLS and Vivado: restore the old values with explicit II (and, if needed, latency) pragmas or directives.
- Alternative: lower the clock uncertainty with `add_clock` / `set_clock`; the more accurate model makes a smaller margin reasonable. Explicit II/latency constraints are the more deterministic of the two routes.
- Check: compare II and latency in the synthesis report before and after the upgrade.

Source: UG1399 v2023.2, Section I: Introduction > Changed Behavior, p. 13.
