Many STL components rely on recursion and heap allocation, which is why the manual treats the STLs as not synthesizable. Standard data types such as std::complex are an exception and can be synthesized; the long double specialization of std::complex is the one the manual marks as unsupported.

Source: UG1399 v2023.2, Ch.7 Unsupported C/C++ Constructs > Standard Template Libraries, p. 148.
