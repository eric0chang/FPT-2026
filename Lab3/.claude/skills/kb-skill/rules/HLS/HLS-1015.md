- Do macro-level architecture first and micro-level optimizations afterwards.
- Timeline: time on the horizontal axis; place each function's execution relative to the others over multiple iterations/invocations to see the expected parallelism.
- Check it against the achieved schedule visualized by the HLS tools, and let the compiler reports steer the optimization.

Source: UG1399 v2023.2, Ch.1 Design Principles > Conclusion - A Prescription for Performance, pp. 37-38.
