Manual's 20×20 multiply-accumulate nest:
- Inner loop pipelined: ~400 cycles, under 250 LUTs and registers, one multiplier; loops auto-flattened.
- Outer loop pipelined: inner loop unrolled into 20 multipliers; 13 cycles, about 2× the logic.
- Function pipelined: both loops unrolled, 400 multipliers and 20 array accesses; 3 cycles, roughly 4× the logic of the first option.
Higher-level pipelining adds many operations to schedule (compile time, memory), and data dependencies or memory ports usually stop the full parallelism from materializing.

Source: UG1399 v2023.2, Ch.3 Loops Primer > Working with Nested Loops, pp. 78-79.
