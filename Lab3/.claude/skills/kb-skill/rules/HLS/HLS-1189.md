---
id: HLS-1189
summary: Do not use loops with more than one exit condition in a DATAFLOW region: a loop containing break or continue cannot be a dataflow task; its only exit must be the loop bound.
tags:

records: []
---
- `continue` counts as an extra exit just like `break`.
- HLS issues a message when it detects this coding style.

Source: UG1399 v2023.2, Ch.10 Optimizing Techniques and Troubleshooting Tips > Limitations of Control-Driven Task-Level Parallelism > Loops with Multiple Exit Conditions, pp. 298-302.
