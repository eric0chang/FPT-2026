- Each listed path is the combinational route between two flip-flops.
- The Resources table is built after Vivado synthesis (DSPs and other logic really instantiated) and can show per module the original variable, source location and originating pragma, which links the path to your C.
- Congestion check: compare the RTL Synthesis and Place & Route timing-path tables. Manual example: logic levels 5 -> 9 and max fanout 64 -> 9366 after P&R.
