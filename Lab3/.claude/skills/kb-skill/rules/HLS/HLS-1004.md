- Look for steps where a later step depends on an earlier one only item by item (e.g. process an item, then write its result): the later step need not wait until all data is processed.
- On a CPU this overlap gains little and is hard to exploit; in custom FPGA hardware producer and consumer execute at the same time with almost no overhead.
- Stream-based links let the consumer begin once the producer begins emitting data, raising parallelism and throughput.
- Keeping the components small and modular also lets you replicate one when more parallelism is needed.

Source: UG1399 v2023.2, Ch.1 Design Principles > Producer-Consumer Paradigm, pp. 27-38.
