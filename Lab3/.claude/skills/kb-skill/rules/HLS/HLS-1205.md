- HLS cannot know the later logic optimizations or routing delays; the Synthesis Summary timing figures (target period, uncertainty, estimated period) are likewise only tool estimates.
- The Function Call Graph is available after C synthesis (estimates) and after co-simulation (measured II/latency plus stalling percentages). Its heat map on II, latency, utilization or stall time (red = highest) points at the critical path, the modules limiting throughput, and imbalanced paths that can lead to FIFO stalls or deadlock.
- Vivado from the HLS component: synthesis-only (vivado.flow=syn, the default) runs faster but lacks implementation detail; implementation adds place & route. `vivado.impl_strategy` affects only this estimation run, not the exported IP or kernel; `vivado.clock` defaults to the component clock. Figures still shift once the block sits among other IP/kernels.
Also covered in UG1399 Ch.12 Building and Running an HLS Component > Running Implementation, pp. 395-396.
